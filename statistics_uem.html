Why It Matters

Why Study Appliance Energy?

🧊
Refrigerators Run 24/7
Single largest residential electricity load — never switch off, account for ~14% of home electricity use
👕
Washers × Hot Water
Top-5 household consumers — draw both electricity AND heat energy; IMEF standards promise savings
📜
Decades of Standards
U.S. NAECA, EU A–G label, ENERGY STAR — do they deliver real savings or just on paper?

Core question: Using the official EPA ENERGY STAR registry, can we statistically prove that modern certified appliances consume significantly less energy than older certified models?

Conclusions

What the Data Tell Us

✅
Modern appliances DO consume significantly less energy
Both t-tests reject H₀: washers −9% (p=0.030), fridges −10% cohort-vs-cohort (p=7.5×10⁻⁷), −70% vs 1981 baseline
🔑
Mechanisms differ by appliance type
Washers: shift toward front-load + smaller drums (IMEF unchanged). Fridges: tech progress outpaces adverse product-mix drift
⚡
Real-world impact: ~6.3 TWh/yr saved per decade
42 kWh/yr difference × 150 M U.S. fridges alone. Standards (not marketing) are the primary driver
⚠️
Limitations to keep in mind
ENERGY STAR only = already efficient floor. Lab kWh ≠ real household use. Year cut-offs set empirically
